Most common issue across every flow: Consistency and Standards

Consistency and Standards accounts for 48 findings combined (26 in Build My System, 22 in Quiz) — it's the #1 heuristic issue in both flows individually, not just overall. Recurring patterns include inconsistent button/CTA styling, mismatched pricing or progress-bar treatments across screens, and components that don't follow the same visual conventions as the rest of the flow. Fixing shared design-system inconsistencies would likely resolve findings in both flows at once.
